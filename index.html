Technological Innovations

Three technologies. One integrated approach.

TopClosure® and regulated negative pressure therapy work together as an integrated approach to early wound closure, built on decades of clinical practice, peer-reviewed research and FDA-cleared devices.

⇤⇥

TopClosure® TRS

The Tension Relief System leverages stress relaxation and mechanical creep to harness the skin's viscoelastic properties, ensuring optimal tension management and enhanced closure of high-tension, difficult-to-heal wounds.

⇣

RNPT

Regulated Negative Pressure Therapy delivers pre-set, controlled negative pressure for removal of exudates and infectious materials, with the FDA-cleared Vcare α® and MeCare α® devices and a patented bleeding control system.

Device Innovations

Our product portfolio

Published Research

Science first: from the lab to leading journals

Led by our founder and CEO, Prof. Moris Topaz, MD, PhD, IVT Medical has built its technologies on more than 15 years of basic science and clinical research. The company's investigational methods, including regulated oxygen-enriched negative pressure treatment (ROI-NPT) and targeted in-situ antibiotic administration (CITA), are the subject of studies published in peer-reviewed journals, including JACC.

  • 35+ peer-reviewed publications across wound closure and wound therapy
  • A NICE medtech innovation briefing in the UK (TopClosure®, MIB97)
  • Research presented at international scientific meetings

ROI-NPT and CITA are investigational methods. They are not FDA-cleared indications of any IVT Medical device, and their safety and effectiveness for those uses have not been established.
